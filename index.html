UNLOCK EARTH

Your world, earned by movement.

Unlock Earth turns your real GPS activities into a permanent collection of the places you have actually moved through.

Download for iPhone Android: join the beta Strava club

Free. A small ad may appear now and then to pay for the servers.

How it works

  • Connect your Strava account once. It is read-only, so Unlock Earth can never change anything on Strava.
  • Your history syncs automatically, including past activities, so on day one you can see every place you have already been.
  • Every qualifying run, ride, hike, ski, paddle and more is checked against real municipality borders. Cover enough distance inside one in a single activity and it is unlocked for good.
  • Be the first person ever to unlock a place and earn a World First.

Where it works

Europe, North, Central and South America, the Caribbean and Australia, with more of the world to come. The USA is covered at county level. Boundaries come from published open data, and every source is listed in the app under Profile, Data sources.

Android beta

The Android app is in closed testing. To join:

  1. Join the tester group with the Google account you use on your phone: Unlock Earth Testers.
  2. Open the testing page on your Android phone and tap "Become a tester".
  3. Install the app from the Play Store link that appears, open Profile and tap Connect to link Strava. Keep "View data about your activities" ticked or nothing can sync.

Your first sync can take from a few minutes to a few hours, because Strava limits how fast apps can load data.
